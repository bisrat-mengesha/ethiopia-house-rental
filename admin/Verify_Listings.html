Verification Guidelines

  1. Review all submitted property information.
  2. Check the owner's details and property documents.
  3. Identify suspicious or fake listings.
  4. Review complaints and reported houses.
  5. Approve valid listings.
  6. Reject invalid listings and record the reason.
  7. Notify the house owner about the decision.

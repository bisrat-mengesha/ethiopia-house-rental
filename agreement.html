5. General Tenancy Terms

  1. Either party giving notice to terminate this tenancy shall provide written notice at least 30 days in advance, or any longer period required by applicable law or agreed in writing.
  2. The tenant shall not sublet, assign, or give another person possession of the property without the landlord's prior written consent. Only the agreed permitted number of occupants may reside in the property.
  3. The tenant shall keep the property reasonably clean and promptly report damage or repairs. The parties shall agree in writing who is responsible for maintenance costs, taking account of applicable law and ordinary wear and tear.
  4. At the end of the tenancy, the tenant shall return the property in its received condition, except for ordinary wear and tear, and subject to the parties' written move-in record.
  5. This agreement is subject to applicable Ethiopian civil tenancy laws and the relevant regional state or city administration rules. Any mandatory legal requirement prevails over a conflicting term in this agreement.

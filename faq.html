Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers about finding a home, listing a property, and renting in Ethiopia.

For tenants

How can I find a house?

Use the search on the home page to filter homes by area, maximum price, type, and number of bedrooms.

What identification do I need to register?

The tenant registration form asks you to select a National ID (Fayda) or passport, enter its number, and upload an image or PDF.

Are rental payments handled through this website?

No. Tenants and owners arrange rent and deposit payments directly. The platform does not collect, hold, or track those payments. Keep a record of any payment you make.

What should I do before paying for a rental?

Verify the property and the owner's authority to rent it, and make sure you understand the agreement before paying. If something seems suspicious, contact us.

Where can I check my rental requests?

Sign in to your tenant account and open My Requests to see their current status.

For property owners and real estate organizations

How do I list a property?

Sign in and open Manage Listings to complete the Add New Listing form.

What information is requested when registering as an owner?

The registration form requests an ID or passport. Organizations can also provide their commercial license or registration number and TIN.

How long does listing verification take?

An administrator reviews new listings before they appear to tenants. Review times may vary.

How do I respond to a rental request?

Open Rental Requests in your owner account and choose Approve or Reject.

How can I view or print a rental agreement?

Open the rental agreement and use your browser's print option to print it or save it as a PDF.
